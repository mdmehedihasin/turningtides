## NASA and USGS data

NASA Earth science data are openly shared without restriction. Landsat data are U.S. public domain. GPM IMERG on the AWS Open Data Registry is listed as CC BY 4.0. NEX-GDDP-CMIP6 is CC0 since September 2022.

- **DS-01 Landsat Collection 2 Level-2** — Landsat Collection 2 Level-2 Science Products courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey; accessed via Microsoft Planetary Computer.
- **DS-02 HLS v2.0** — Masek, J. et al. (2021). HLS Operational Land Imager Surface Reflectance and TOA Brightness Daily Global 30m v2.0 (and HLS Sentinel-2 MSI v2.0). NASA EOSDIS LP DAAC.
- **DS-03 ECOSTRESS LST** — Hook, S., Hulley, G. et al. ECOSTRESS Tiled Land Surface Temperature and Emissivity Instantaneous L2 Global 70 m V002. NASA EOSDIS LP DAAC. doi:10.5067/ECOSTRESS/ECO_L2T_LSTE.002

- **DS-10 NASADEM** — NASA JPL (2020). NASADEM Merged DEM Global 1 arc second V001. LP DAAC. doi:10.5067/MEaSUREs/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001

- **DS-18 NISAR soil moisture** — NASA/ISRO NISAR Project. NISAR L3 Soil Moisture (SME2), PROVISIONAL Version 1. ASF DAAC. https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/asf-nisar-l3-sme2-provisional-v1-1

## Software

The web app bundles Preact, @preact/signals and the d3 modules d3-array, d3-geo, d3-scale and d3-shape (MIT or ISC licences). The data pipeline (not part of this published build) uses open-source Python packages, each under its own licence. The 3D Atlas uses MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause) and three.js (MIT).
